## 3. Statutory County Relaxation Justifications

### 3.1 Clause s.18: Front Setback Relaxation (6.0 m Requirement)
- **Application Context:** Nairobi City County Development Control Regulations allow street-front commercial podiums in designated medium-to-high density mixed-use zones (e.g., Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Westlands, Pangani, Parklands).
- **Planning Rationale:**
  1. **Active Street Frontage & Urban Safety:** Ground-floor convenience retail (16.7 m²) and the manned guardhouse (6.6 m²) provide eyes on the street, continuous passive surveillance, and active pedestrian animation, eliminating blank boundary walls that harbor insecurity.
  2. **Controlled Access & Traffic Safety:** The 3.48 m car entry portal incorporates a 5.0 m vehicle deceleration zone at ≤ 1:25 grade behind the property line, ensuring cars decelerate and stop completely within the plot boundary before initiating ramp descent.
  3. **Visual Articulation of Upper Storeys:** Residential levels (L1–L4) are visually and climatically screened with vertical timber brise-soleil fins projecting 300 mm, establishing a rhythmic architectural façade that softens the streetscape.
- **Relief Requested:** Waiver of 6.0 m front setback under Section 58 of PLUPA 2019 to permit zero ground-floor setback for commercial and security uses.

### 3.2 Clause s.19: Side Setback Relaxation (1.5 m Requirement)
- **Application Context:** Standard 50 ft × 100 ft plot geometry in urban infill neighborhoods.
- **Planning & Structural Rationale:**
  1. **Plot Width Constraint:** The plot is exactly 15.24 m wide. Deducting 1.5 m side setbacks on both boundaries leaves only 12.24 m buildable width. This width is physically incapable of supporting two residential wings plus a compliant internal courtyard (7.14 m width).
  2. **100% Fire Separation (Zero Opening Party Walls):** Both boundary walls are solid 200 mm machine-cut stone rendered and 300 mm reinforced concrete retaining walls, with zero openings, doors, or windows facing adjacent properties. This provides a certified **2-hour Fire Resistance Rating (FRR)** exceeding LN 47/2024 s.129 and s.132.
  3. **Zero Stormwater Encroachment:** The roof slab is designed with perimeter parapets (1.10 m high) and internal valley gutters directing 100% of rainwater runoff into internal downpipes, the basement rainwater harvesting tank (27 m³), and the courtyard bioswale. Zero rainwater discharges onto neighboring land.
  4. **Superior Daylighting & Ventilation:** By concentrating open space into a single central 112.1 m² courtyard (exceeding code by 220%), all habitable rooms achieve daylight levels superior to narrow 1.5 m side alleys.
- **Relief Requested:** Permission for zero-lot-line party-wall construction on North and South boundaries.

## 4. Technical Calculations & Engineering Verifications

### 4.1 Window Area Uplift Calculation (Clause s.143(2))
- **Code Clause:** LN 47/2024 s.143(2) mandates a 5% increase in glazed area for every 300 mm that an overhead balcony projects beyond the face of the window.
- **Geometry:**
  * Balcony cantilever projection: 1,200 mm = 4 × 300 mm.
  * Required glazing uplift: 4 × 5% = **+20%**.
  * Base requirement (s.143(1)): 10% of room floor area.
  * Total required window area: 10% + 20% = **30.0% of room floor area**.
- **Room Dimensions (Left-Wing 2-Bed Living/Dining Room):**
  * Room length = 6.20 m, width = 3.60 m → Net floor area = **22.32 m²**.
  * Minimum required window area at 30%: 22.32 × 0.30 = **6.70 m²**.
- **Implemented Model Specification (v3.1):**
  * Window width widened from 3.00 m to **3.50 m**.
  * Window height = **2.40 m** (floor-to-ceiling sliding glazed wall).
  * Provided glazed area = 3.50 m × 2.40 m = **8.40 m²**.
  * **Provided Window-to-Floor Ratio = 8.40 / 22.32 = 37.63% > 30.0% (PASS).**

### 4.2 Staircase Ergonomics & Code Check (Clause s.205)
- **Code Limits:** Risers 150–180 mm, Treads 250–320 mm, Flight Width ≥ 1,200 mm.
- **Floor-to-Floor Height:** 3,000 mm (L1 to L2, L2 to L3, etc.).
- **Calculated Parameters:**
  * Number of risers: **17 risers**.
  * Riser height ($R$): 3,000 mm / 17 = **176.47 mm** (PASS: 150 ≤ $R$ ≤ 180).
  * Tread depth ($T$): **280.00 mm** (PASS: 250 ≤ $T$ ≤ 320).
  * Flight clear width ($W$): **1,200.00 mm** (PASS: $W \ge 1,200$).
  * Blondel Stair Formula check: $2R + T = 2(176.47) + 280 = \mathbf{632.9\text{ mm}}$ (Optimum comfort range: 600–650 mm — PERFECT).
  * Pitch angle: $\arctan(176.47 / 280) = \mathbf{32.2^\circ}$ (Ideal comfort slope).

### 4.3 Basement Ventilation Sizing (Clause s.156)
- **Basement Dimensions:** 15.24 m width × 23.00 m depth × 2.90 m clear height.
- **Gross Volume:** 1,016 m³ | Net Air Volume (less structure/tanks): **650 m³**.
- **Ventilation Demands:**
  * Normal Operation (6 Air Changes/Hour): $6 \times 650 = \mathbf{3,900\text{ m}^3\text{/h}}\ (1.083\text{ m}^3\text{/s})$.
  * Smoke Extract / Ramp Queue (10 Air Changes/Hour): $10 \times 650 = \mathbf{6,500\text{ m}^3\text{/h}}\ (1.806\text{ m}^3\text{/s})$.
- **Extract Shaft Geometry:**
  * 2 concrete shafts @ 0.80 m × 0.80 m = $0.64\text{ m}^2$ each.
  * Total shaft cross-sectional area: $2 \times 0.64 = \mathbf{1.28\text{ m}^2}$.
- **Air Velocities:**
  * At 6 ACH: $1.083 / 1.28 = \mathbf{0.85\text{ m/s}}$.
  * At 10 ACH: $1.806 / 1.28 = \mathbf{1.41\text{ m/s}}$.
  * **Acoustic Criterion:** Maximum permissible velocity in residential shaft = 5.0 m/s.
  * **Result:** At 1.41 m/s, air velocity is well below 5.0 m/s, guaranteeing silent acoustic operation and minimal fan static pressure head.
